## Observations (disclosed; not blockers)

- **O1 — duplicate proposals within one sweep.** `existing` is snapshotted before the pair loop
  (`crates/wm-gen3-core/src/ops.rs:751`), so one endpoint pair is re-proposed once per shared rare
  token passing the rule: A1 shows 5 relations (ids 0–4) on the same `(src=1, dst=0)`; recall
  applies the last (id 4). Direction and ordering are unaffected; relation counts are inflated.
  Flagged for a future registration (within-sweep refresh of `existing` or relation-identity
  dedup). No verdict, gate, or claim changes.
- **O2 — gate activation shown, not outcome differences.** On this synthetic fixture the four B
  runs returned identical result sets; the count/floor/off contrast is visible in the journals
  (`projection.gate` fired sets, candidate counts, projection config), consistent with C3's
  scope: the canary demonstrates the declared activation *behaviour*, not an outcome claim.
- **O3 — canary record included.** `run.end.records` = 3 in A runs includes the simulated record
  planted by the closure canary; semantics unaffected.
