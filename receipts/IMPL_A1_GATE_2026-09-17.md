## 2. What was implemented

- Identity index `(content sha256, source, kind)` on `Substrate`, **hydrated at open** from the
  durable store (cross-process persistence).
- `remember` refuses an already-present identity with `remember.refusal` reason
  **`duplicate_exact`** (fields: `content_sha256`, `source`, `existing_id`); **no second record is
  written**; the existing record is not mutated.
- Provenance-scoped: identical bytes with a different `source`/`kind` are admitted as distinct
  records (the "same content, different context" adversarial case).
- No numeric parameters; no ranking-path changes (a gate, not a scorer).
