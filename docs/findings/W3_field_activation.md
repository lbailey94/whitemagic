## Selection history

Gen1 (v24.3 planner) wired the channel inline over the same association tables the graph walk used
(0.5 downweight, probe 0.15 vs engine 0.05); Gen2 ported the engine to Rust
(`WMv9/docs/GAP_ANALYSIS_v26_v4.md:129`) but moved its live consumers offline — dream Oracle, Gan
Ying sweep, `graph.propagate` — with the recall graph-walk knob defaulted off
(`docs/findings/W2_gen2_source_map.md`). Net fate: **read-side channel (Gen1) → offline/tool
(Gen2)**; decay machinery in both, tuning evidence in neither.

## Adversarial cases
1. **Only-reachable-by-edge target.** v26 adds it to `graph_score` at ≤ `0.4·0.5/(60+rank+1)` vs
   full lexical+semantic sums (search_planner.py:398–425); Gen2 has no recall-path propagation.
   Expected: no ordering change. Untested in both.
2. **Hub crowding.** v26 caps 30 raw neighbors per galaxy ordered by strength (337–344) and 200
   primed nodes; Gen2 `graph.propagate` returns top 50 (graph.rs:448–451). Weak-but-correct paths
   drop before decay can matter.
3. **Cold/None scores.** v26 priming of NULL `neuro_score` becomes 1.0 via COALESCE (421); Gen2
   ripple/priority gates require `neuro_score >= 0.5` (neural.rs:247–258) and protection at
   `importance >= 0.9` (retention.rs:238–245) — cold regions are simultaneously over-boosted
   (v26) and never selected (Gen2).
4. **Decay asymmetry.** v26 increments `recall_count` on priming with no counter-decay while the
   24 h daemon judges by 30 d half-life (decay_daemon.py:24–28; sqlite_schema.py:92) — ordering
   after priming is a function of wall-clock, not evidence.
5. **Saturation without decay.** Gen2 gardens are stimulated every daemon cycle and `tick_decay`
   is never called (gardens.rs:1013–1020, zero callers); any repeatedly matched garden saturates
   at 1.0. A test should assert bounded, actually decaying activations on the live path.
## Ablation ideas
- v26: `spreading_activation=False` (retrieval_plan.py:100) and separately `graph_walk_weight=0`
  (disables the stage, search_planner.py:314) on a corpus with known multi-hop items; log
  `retrieval_channels` (search_planner.py:437) to count how often SA is even a tie-breaker.
- Gen2: exclude `graph.propagate`; zero `w_spreading` (gan_ying.rs:389); attach vs omit Smarana on
  `DreamCycle` (server.rs:527); call `tick_decay` in the daemon loop vs not.
- Cross-generation: hold the candidate set fixed and compare "graph adds candidates" vs "graph
  re-scores only" — both do the former inside a fused score, so the propagation contribution is
  inseparable from candidate expansion without this ablation.
