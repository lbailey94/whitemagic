## 5. The Submission Architecture

### Video (3 minutes) — The Emotional Hook
- **Structure:** Act I (cosmic → human, 30s) → Act II (cascade of 7 visions, 110s) → Doubt (6s) → Act III (candles + child + montage, 34s)
- **Visual approach:** Anthology art-house — 14 distinct 2D illustrated styles, real footage only for candles and child
- **Score:** Generated first in Google Flow Music; everything edits to the music
- **The "one detail that proves it's real":** Each vision flash includes one specific technical detail that demonstrates producibility

### Treatment (12 pages) — The Intellectual Proof
Structured as a proof-of-concept document, not just a story pitch:
1. **The World** (2-3 pages): The cascade timeline, each vision expanded with technical specificity
2. **The Story** (3-4 pages): How the 3-minute trailer expands into a 90-minute feature
3. **The Technology** (2-3 pages): Specific technologies from SFW 2.0 — ecodroids, AI symbiosis, helium-3 chain, Labor Zero, mycoremediation
4. **The Impact** (1-2 pages): Why this film matters — the Overton window shift, the self-fulfilling prophecy
5. **The Proof** (1 page): WhiteMagic as evidence — prescience claims, the timeline of predictions that came true

### Cover Sheet (1 page) — The Credibility Anchor
- **Logline** (locked): "At the crossroads of human history, a circle of diverse voices shares a cascading vision — each possibility building on the last — from the courage to choose differently, to a civilization spanning the stars. But first, one small flame must decide not to go out."
- **Synopsis** (300 words): The story, compressed
- **Personal Statement** (300 words): The prescience pitch — "I didn't just imagine this future. I started building it. Parts of it came true."
