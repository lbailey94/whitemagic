## 4. Findings

1. **Two arcs led the field, one is leading it right now.** Governance/audit
   (rows 1, 2, 8, 9: 15–47 wk, STRONG) and memory-lifecycle operations
   (row 7 dreaming; distillation; the S11 integrity pack) ran ahead of the
   field's shipped mechanisms. The **currently unclaimed lead** is the
   integrity/security arc (rows 13, 14, 16, 17: sealed provenance, signed
   identity + quarantine, kernel confinement, shipped containment controls)
   — exactly the axis W7 found vendors refuse to occupy, and exactly what
   ratified Slice A extends.
2. **Persistence primitives converged fastest** — Zep (bi-temporal,
   2025-01) and A-MEM (note evolution, 2025-02) precede some WM lifecycle
   mechanics; the field caught up on storage mechanics quickest. The
   differentiator was never storage; it was governance and verification.
3. **Distribution is the structural lag.** The field-facing surface is one
   ephemeral PyPI publish (2026-06-21) and one JSON API endpoint
   (prescience.json). The Five-Layer Discoverability Stack was designed
   (07-04) and never built. The gardener's human garden did not materialize;
   the agent garden did. If messaging priority (ratified) is to matter,
   this is the gap it closes — with the language constraints from W7
   (verifiable provenance, containment framing, no SOTA without
   independent runs).
4. **The forecasting discipline is itself the differentiator.** No surveyed
   vendor maintains a falsifiable, Brier-calibrated, dated claim register.
   Rows 18's independence finding (vendor numbers vs independent reruns)
   makes the discipline marketable as methodology, not just history.
5. **For messaging, cite rows 1, 7, 8, 13, 14, 16, 17** — governance,
   dreaming, and the integrity arc — with dates and evidence grades, under
   the honesty rules above. The Oct-2024 origin story stays in the memoir
   where the canon already marks it myth; public chronology anchors on
