# Gen3 — Nucleus-freeze criteria (draft)

**Status:** criteria draft · 2026-09-17 · **reviewed in session; four-part snapshot restructure
and two-door admission/disposition split applied** (§1–§2). Remains a draft until the freeze is
performed. Docs-only: performs no freeze, names no frozen hashes, changes no gate. Companion to
`PHASE3_ENTRY_DECISION.md` and `PHASE3_COMPILE_PASS.md`.

Purpose: by the Phase-3/Phase-4 boundary, Gen3 must have an explicit **nucleus snapshot** — the
pinned part of the organism that Phase-4 re-expression may inherit from but never dissolve into
Gen1/Gen2. Within it, the constitutional core is Tier 1, the earned mechanism set is empirical
(§2a), and the statutory baseline stays externally amendable (Charter §2 tiers). The ruling this
serves: *Gen1/Gen2 provide capabilities and evidence, not architecture.* This file defines what
may enter the nucleus, what may not, and how the freeze is performed, verified, and amended.

---

## 1. What is frozen — the nucleus snapshot (four parts)

The freeze produces one artefact, `NUCLEUS.md`, that keeps four kinds of things visibly
distinct. All four are pinned; they are **not** the same kind of law (Charter §2 tiers).

```
FROZEN GEN3 NUCLEUS SNAPSHOT
│
├── Constitutional core                  ← Tier 1
│   └── the ten invariants               amendment: Charter §4 only
│
├── Earned cognitive nucleus             ← admission: §2a (empirical)
│   └── records + provenance (H4/H6), R/currentness + structural strata,
│       selection explanations, journal-as-evidence
│
├── Statutory baseline                   ← Tier 2: versioned, externally amendable
│   └── D2 score policy, candidacy rule v1 (divisor 20 / floor 2 / budget 200k),
│       τ = 0.694, audit protocol versions (v1.2 / v1.3),
│       switch inventory + defaults, budgets / horizons
│
└── Reproducibility pins                 ← historical anchors, not cognition
    └── artefact hashes, corpus manifests, the 9.1.7 reference animal
```

Reading rules:

- **Constitutional core** is Tier 1: no adaptive write path reaches it; amendment only via
  Charter §4 (proposal → canary demonstration → operator ratification → receipt).
- **Earned cognitive nucleus** is the mechanism set Phase-3 evidence earns; admission is §2a;
  replacement requires new evidence on the same terms, never preference.
- **Statutory baseline** is Tier 2: explicitly versioned settings, changeable by authorized
  external action without re-ratifying the constitution.
- **Reproducibility pins** make the snapshot checkable; they are anchors, not capabilities,
  and are regenerated whenever a constituent changes.

τ = 0.694 is not constitutional identity in the sense that *"simulation cannot become world
evidence"* is; the snapshot must not flatten that difference.

## 2. Admission and disposition (two doors)

### 2a. Nucleus admission — mechanisms / primitives

An item may enter the **earned cognitive nucleus** only if **all** hold:

1. **Earned by evidence** — cross-ecology positive on ≥ 2 independent corpora; or a
   machine-tested closure/canary with a live refusal (constitutional items sit in the core
   already, not here).
2. **Reproducible** — every parameter pinned; counts regenerated from commands, never authored;
   no benchmark-derived thresholds.
3. **Ablatable with behavioral teeth** — disabling it changes a measured outcome (no inert
   passengers; the dispersion and collinearity failures are the precedent).
4. **Minimal** — cannot be expressed as a composition of already-admitted primitives
   (anti-bloat law, canon §7). Compositions enter as recipes, not as new classes.
5. **Audited** — survives the audit protocol; flags and failures published, never deleted.
6. **Inspected** — `inspect` can answer tier ownership, read/write authorities, and the
   selection explanation for it.

### 2b. Compile-pass disposition — preserved implementations and retirements

Preserved implementations (`PRESERVE IMPLEMENTATION → link`), retirements (`RETIRE`), and
never-fairly-tried ancestors (`EXPERIMENT`) do **not** enter the nucleus. They earn their
disposition in the Phase-3 compile pass — evidence or lineage ruling — and are recorded in the
**frozen historical record** beside the snapshot. A correctly-evidenced retirement does not
become a nucleus entry by being correct; it is a disposition, not a primitive.

## 3. Exclusion and deferral rules

1. **Rejected stays rejected.** T8 cross-vocabulary bridging, hard pair gating (R1), dispersion
   weighting, and the 0.01-floor activation policy are outside the nucleus permanently unless a
   new registration + fresh holdout + ledger action re-opens them.
2. **Conditional lenses are slots, not stages.** S (projection) may enter only as a *declared
   optional lens slot*, with its confirmed `count < 2` activation scoped to state-resolution
   questions. It may not become a mandatory pipeline stage; its activation policy is not
   general.
3. **Open boundaries stay outside.** The ≥2-candidate insufficiency boundary, TIE dynamics,
   projection-agreement ordering, multi-view selection, field dynamics, Gardens/Engines
   recipes, Geneseed/inheritance, and the deferred representation stack (Arrow/Julia/iceoryx2)
   each have a named gate and do not enter by proximity.
4. **Infrastructure is not nucleus.** LMDB/Tantivy/JSONL storage, process model, and
   distribution mechanics are infrastructure: allowed and required for operation, but
   swappable without re-ratification — they are not constitutional identity.
5. **Nothing symbolic dispatches** (Charter §3.10) — already law; restated because Phase 4 will
   re-encounter the symbolic families.

## 4. Freeze procedure

1. **Compile the artefact** — `NUCLEUS.md` per §1 (four-part snapshot: constitutional core ·
   earned cognitive nucleus · statutory baseline · reproducibility pins; scope footnotes attach
   to the snapshot as a whole).
2. **Verify the pins** — regenerate counts and hashes; a mismatch is a finding, not a re-pin.
3. **Canary demonstration** — for each protective claim the freeze leans on, show the behaviour:
   closure canaries remain live; R ablation changes ordering; the count gate ablated reverts to
   the declared always-on/off behaviour; provenance chains complete at result granularity.
   Two canaries added (2026-09-17, earned by the wave-0 runtime findings): **round-trip canary** —
   every admitted relation kind/endpoint set persists and reloads intact (the typed-associations
   lesson: v26's ontology was erased at persistence); **reachable-effectful acceptance** — every
   admitted primitive demonstrates one reachable path from a verb to a journal-visible effect
   (the wiring lesson: tours over 662 tools where half the organs were unreachable).
4. **Operator ratification** — explicit, per the Charter §4 procedure; AI sessions prepare and
   attest, never ratify.
5. **Receipt** — `receipts/` entry with the artefact hash, verified pins, session attestation.
6. **Timing** — after the Phase-3 verdict pass is ratified; before any Phase-4 re-expression
   wave begins. The frozen 9.1.7 control remains the reference animal throughout.

## 5. Amendment rule

- Amendments are additive history: proposal → canary demonstration → operator ratification →
  receipt (Charter §4). No emergency suspension clause.
- The nucleus is expected to be **rarely** amended; the next amendment should come from an
  experiment breaking a nucleus entry, not from design prose.
- Phase-4 fusion may expose missing primitives; each enters through the §2 admission criteria
  and pays the same toll the `count < 2` policy paid (registration + fresh holdout + ledger) —
  no convenience exceptions.

## 6. Acceptance of the freeze

- Every entry cites evidence or a lineage ruling; nothing admitted by taste.
- Every scope footnote names what is *not* claimed.
- Every pin verifies; a failed verification is disclosed and blocks the freeze.
- The operator ratifies; the receipt lands; the Charter's fail-closed budget rules apply to the
  freeze ceremony itself (bounded sessions).

## 7. Non-goals

No code, no primitive additions, no verb-basis expansion, no Phase-4 scheduling, no
re-litigation of falsified claims, no new thresholds.
